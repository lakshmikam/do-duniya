# Playing Do Duniya

Two investigators work the same mystery from complementary positions. One explores physical scenes and questions people on site; the other checks logs, authorizations and the people responsible for them.

1. Choose a role and invite your partner with the room code.
2. Read your role and the shared chapter question. One investigates the scene; the other checks records.
3. Tap a numbered clue, witness or named action to open it directly.
4. Each discovered clue immediately appears in **both notebooks**. There is no manual sharing step.
5. Compare what you found in Partner Link. Type or speak a completed turn; different selected languages get an AI translation. Use Hear messages for Timbre playback. Original messages remain visible. Raw radio is separate and untranslated.
6. When enough relevant clues are found, choose the three answers. The notebook is included automatically; both players confirm.

## Test what witnesses say

Meeting a witness records their claims in the notebook's **Statements** section, visible to both players. A claim is not an established fact. In the conversation, open **Test this account with evidence**, choose an exact statement and a discovered notebook passage, then present it. A correct contradiction can change the witness's account and earn new testimony. A supporting passage can confirm an honest statement. Unrelated evidence does not advance the story.

Some changes need evidence from both roles or another witness's admission. Name the source when talking to your partner: “The receipt disagrees with her story. Can you check which clock its times use?” New testimony files itself in both notebooks without adding more object markers to the scene. Freeform conversation history remains private.

Repeated different wrong comparisons can make a witness go quiet for 45 seconds. Check another lead and return; no evidence is lost and the case never fails permanently. Repeating the identical wrong comparison does not add pressure. The optional shared hints help identify a missing comparison without naming the culprit.

After a correct reconstruction, an optional epilogue asks how the team will close the case. Both players must choose the same option. Either can change their choice until agreement; then the ending is recorded. Solo rehearsal needs one choice. A wrong reconstruction can be reopened with discoveries intact.

## Simplified controls

Tap an actor badge, numbered clue or named action; no separate E/Interact step is needed. Keyboard users
can Tab to those buttons and activate them normally. There are no hiding, running,
keepsake or scenery-reaction controls. One introduction explains both roles.
The separate activity sidebar and final evidence-selection checklist are removed.

Character voice chat and generated scene announcements remain. Start with sound
for automatic replies, or play quietly. Partner radio is optional inside Partner
Link; typed messages work without a microphone. Three optional team hints live inside the bound notebook, rather than directing every move from the front screen.
Private conversation history stays private. Witness claims are shared as statements, while only actually discovered case clues enter the evidence pages. Unrevealed testimony and the solution remain server-only.

## Limits

The interface is mostly English; translated story passages remain unreviewed.
Speech services can take several seconds or hit configured call limits. Authored
English controls and evidence challenges complete both mysteries without models. Freeform questions use model topic routing when available and an explicitly authored fallback when unavailable. Radio still requires both players to enable it. Timed dispatches add atmosphere; pause stops the story clock and overtime does not end the case. Automated and local browser checks do not establish real-phone microphone or two-person usability.

One tab controls each player. A duplicate tab stops and asks before taking over. For two players, join under different names using the room code. Refresh and start a new room after an update to the test service.
