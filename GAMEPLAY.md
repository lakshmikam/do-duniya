# Playing Do Duniya

The current version simplifies the two-player loop after playtesting.

1. Choose a role and invite your partner with the room code.
2. Read **Your job** and your partner’s job. Both see the same chapter question.
3. Tap the task button, a clue, or a person. The investigator approaches and opens it automatically.
4. Each discovered clue immediately appears in **both notebooks**. There is no manual sharing step.
5. Read **What to talk about**. Ask that question over radio, type a reply, or send it with one tap.
6. When enough relevant clues are found, choose the three answers. The notebook is included automatically; both players confirm.

For the train case, the station investigator checks the scene and finds Arun.
The records investigator checks the announcement, store route, keys and pickup
approval. Finding the route opens the other player’s next location automatically.
The extra electrical power-switch puzzle has been removed.

## Simplified controls

Tap a named person or clue; no separate E/Interact step is needed. Keyboard users
can Tab to those buttons and activate them normally. There are no hiding, running,
keepsake or scenery-reaction controls. One introduction explains both roles.
The separate activity sidebar and final evidence-selection checklist are removed.

Character voice chat and generated scene announcements remain. Start with sound
for automatic replies, or play quietly. Partner radio is optional inside Partner
Link; text communication and suggested questions work without a microphone.
Private conversation history stays private. Only actually discovered case clues
enter the team notebook; unrevealed testimony and the solution remain server-only.

## Limits

The interface is mostly English; translated story passages remain unreviewed.
Speech services can take several seconds or hit configured call limits. Authored
English controls can complete both mysteries without models. Radio still requires
both players to enable it. Desktop/browser and real-phone playtesting are not
claimed while Work browser control is disabled.
